Serving Suggestions

Exceptional when paired with hot, freshly steamed rice, instant ramen, or wrapped around slices of grilled pork belly (samgyeopsal).

Storage Instructions

Keep refrigerated. Can be enjoyed fresh for a strong, sharp bite, or allowed to mature over a few weeks to develop a deep, complex, and classic sour flavor profile.
