Serving Suggestions

Highly recommended alongside deep, rich Korean soups (like Seolleongtang or Galbitang) or fatty roasted meats where its tangy mustard punch shines bright.

Storage Instructions

Keep refrigerated. This type of kimchi benefits beautifully from patient aging; its bold pungency mellows out into an incredibly satisfying, rich umami over time.
